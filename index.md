The Interactive Narrative Challenge (INCH for shot) is an international research
competition and showcase that evaluates storytelling algorithms.

From
[Choose Your Own Adventure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choose_Your_Own_Adventure)
books, to
[Dungeons & Dragons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeons_%26_Dragons),
to video games like
[80 Days](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/80_Days_(2014_video_game), artists and
researches have developed many ways to tell stories that adapt to a player's
choices. Storytelling algorithms have existed for
[at least 50 years](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/1624435.1624452), but because
each system tends to be created for its own game, it can be hard to compare them
to one another. The INCH showcase is an attempt to evaluate many different
interactive storytelling techniques in the same story world with the same
interface.

## How to Participate

INCH will use [Tandem Tales](http://tandemtales.net/), a platform for playing
short text-based interactive stories. Every story has two participants: a player
who controls one character and a game master who controls the other characters
and the world. Either participant can be a human (playing via a web browser) or
an automated agent. Tandem Tales makes it easy to match a person with a person,
a person with an agent, or even an agent with an agent.

To participate in INCH:
- Fill out the Intent to Participate form by DEADLINE.
- Develop a game master agent for [The Crown](#about-the-crown-story-world)
  story world.
- Agents should be submitted as [Docker](https://www.docker.com/) containers,
  which means you can use almost any technology stack to build your agent.
- Submit your completed agent by DEADLINE.

In the [Resource](#resources) section we have links to example agents in
multiple programming languages, details on *The Crown* story world, training
data, and other things you might find useful.

## Why Participate?

We will do a large-scale evaluation of your agent with both human judges and
several automated metrics. We'll evaluate your work against several baseline
techniques and the submissions of other participants. This showcase is an
opportunity for a controlled, repeatable, measurable evaluation of many
different storytelling approaches.

Participants will also be invited to submit a peer-reviewed paper for the first
INCH Workshop, to be held at the 23rd AAAI conference on Artificial Intelligence
and Interactive Digital Entertainment (AIIDE).

There will be plenty of awards to win, bragging rights, and a treasure trove of
data generated by this showcase. The results will be announced at the 2027 AIIDE
conference.

## Showcase Over Competition

The diversity of the games and storytelling research community is one of its
great strengths. We want a way to compare one agent to another, but we
*do not want* to over-focus our research community on specific metrics on one
particular definition of a "good story."

INCH is designed as as a showcase rather than a competition. We will evaluate
many different metrics and give many awards. There is *intentionally* no Best
in Show award. Our goal is to celebrate many kinds of success, not to name one
overall winner.
